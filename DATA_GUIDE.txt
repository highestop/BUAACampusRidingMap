Map alignment
- Register annotated satellite images to local metric coordinates using ground-level junctions and corresponding road features, then convert to WGS84.
  Do not interpret Amap image pixels or GCJ-02 readings directly as WGS84. Image registration and manual annotations still have uncertainty.
- Match ordinary routes using along-line samples, road headings, and local continuity, with a maximum candidate displacement of 18 m.
  Prefer nearby road junctions with matching branch directions. Incident segments share exactly the same endpoint coordinates.
- Reduce alignment strength locally if it would introduce unintended crossings. Preserve nearby distinct roads and real loops.
- S066 and S068 have no corresponding mapped road. Keep their route shapes and reconnect shared endpoints without forcing them onto unrelated roads.
- S004 is partially matched. The map_alignment fields record match coverage and status.
- This is a representative network derived from activity and map evidence, not a surveying product.

Field conventions
1. Geographic coordinates use WGS84 EPSG:4326 and are stored as [longitude, latitude].
2. geometry_xy stores metres in EPSG:32650 (UTM zone 50N), relative to metadata.local_xy_origin_utm_m.
3. segment.coordinates contains the complete ordered polyline. point_a and point_b define storage order only; all segments are undirected.
4. length_m is the sum of WGS84 ellipsoidal distances along successive polyline vertices, not the straight-line endpoint distance.
5. degree counts distinct segment IDs. The same endpoint pair can have multiple geometrically distinct routes.
6. is_user_corrected identifies user-requested map corrections. geometry_method distinguishes image registration, junction refinement, and ordinary basemap alignment.
7. geometry_is_estimate and length_is_estimate mark estimates. Decimal precision does not imply positional accuracy.
8. supporting_gps_record_indices are zero-based indexes into raw_track, not indexes into all FIT messages.
   Affected samples are reassigned to the nearest corrected segment within 18 m. Proximity support does not prove that the entire corrected route was traversed.
9. S100's is_inferred flag describes the origin of its added geometry; its connection was confirmed by the user. geometry_method records its current construction method.
10. Retired point and segment IDs are not reused. Metadata records retired IDs and the regional correction history.

Historical initial extraction
The source FIT contained 3,662 valid GPS samples and reported an activity distance of 16,680.50 m. GPS polyline length and FIT distance are different measurements.
Initial processing used a 5 m trajectory buffer, 1 m raster centerlines, narrow-gap merging, compact junction clusters, and iterative dead-end removal.
The approximately 10 m merge tolerance produced 64 points and 99 segments before S100 and subsequent map corrections were added.
The 8/10/12 m experiments in metadata.sensitivity apply only to the original extraction before manual corrections.
